# Illustrative accommodation photography

These three locally bundled photographs are stock imagery used to create a visual mood for the stay enquiry page. They are not photographs of Ratan Mahal, and must not be presented as actual rooms, facilities or accommodation available at the sanctuary. The booking page asks visitors to confirm current details with the local team.

Pexels states that its photos and videos can be downloaded and used for free on websites; attribution is not required, but is included here as a courtesy. See the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/).
